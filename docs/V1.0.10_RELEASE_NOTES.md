# Hearthstone HUD v1.0.10

## SteamOS · 地下竞技场重新选牌

- 补选时，左侧显示三张候选牌的 HearthArena 评分并高亮最高分，右侧显示当前牌组。
- 确认完整的 35 张牌组后，左侧候选面板收起。右侧保留每张牌的评分，并标记建议移除的 5 张低分牌。重复牌按实际份数参与推荐，缺少评分的牌显示「暂无评分」。
- 每移除一张，右侧标题和推荐都按最新牌组更新。剩余 34 张时需移除 4 张，回到 30 张时清除标记。修复了移除期间可能出现的 `35/30` 计数。

## SteamOS · Underground Arena redrafts

- During the five extra picks, the left panel shows HearthArena ratings for all three offers and highlights the highest score. The current deck stays on the right.
- When the full 35-card deck is confirmed, the offer panel closes. The deck panel keeps each card’s rating visible and highlights up to five of the lowest-rated copies. Cards without a rating are excluded from suggestions.
- After each cut, the title and highlights update from the current deck. At 34 cards, four cuts remain. At 30, the highlights clear. The removal screen no longer shows `35/30`.
