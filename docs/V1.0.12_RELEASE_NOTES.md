# Hearthstone HUD v1.0.12

## SteamOS · 地下竞技场

移除到 30 张时，右侧保留完整牌组并清除高亮。撤销一次回到 31 张时，HUD 会重新高亮 1 张建议移除的牌。反复移除或撤销时，推荐随当前牌组更新，以本轮首次确认的 35 张牌及各牌份数为基准。

## Underground Arena on SteamOS

At 30 cards, the deck stays visible and the cut highlights clear. Undo a cut to return to 31 cards and one suggestion reappears. Repeated cuts and undos update the suggestions against the original 35-card pool, including duplicate copies.
